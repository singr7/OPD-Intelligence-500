# SESSION-INTAKE-CONTINUITY — Returning, Referred, And Follow-up Patients

Type: future planning only

## Goal

Plan a clinically safe intake front door that does not assume every arrival is a
new anonymous consultation.

The planning session must address three observed gaps:

1. The live “what you have told us” rail must remain visible through read-back and
   confirmation instead of disappearing at the point the patient verifies it.
2. “What else do you want to tell the doctor?” must be retained and surfaced to
   the doctor as patient-provided context, with an explicit decision about whether
   it can influence summary presentation but never silently change routing,
   red flags, or deterministic clinical rules.
3. A returning or referred patient needs an arrival-intent choice before a full
   intake: new problem, planned follow-up, returning with tests/results, referred
   by another doctor, or another clearly defined path. The plan must cover safe
   patient matching, linkage to the prior visit/referral/appointment, when a short
   update is sufficient, and when a fresh intake is still required.

Do not implement this from the brief alone. The planning session needs clinical,
registration, privacy, queue, offline, and audit acceptance criteria first.
